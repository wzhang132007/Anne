PathAble
Accessibility-First Navigation
Flow 1: First-Time Onboarding
Step 1: App Launch
• Clear accessibility value statement
• "Safe, step-free navigation for confident travel"
• Establish trust immediately
Step 2: Account Creation
• Minimal input required
• Low friction setup
• Quick access to value
Step 3: Mobility Profile
• Select mobility needs (wheelchair, slow walking, etc.)
• Choose sensory preferences
• Set default routing constraints
• "I don't want to explain every time"
Step 4: Confirmation
• Review preferences
• Understand routing impact
• "Routes may be longer but safer"
Flow 2: Start Navigation
Step 1: Destination Search
• Enter destination
• Recent locations
• Promise: "Safest route based on your needs"
Step 2: Route Preview
• Step-free route by default
• Safety score, incline, crossing difficulty
• View alternatives & accessibility layers
Step 3: Route Selection
• Confirm route
• Lock accessibility constraints
• Predictability over speed
Step 4: Turn-by-Turn
• Clear visual instructions
• Voice + haptic cues
• Monitor hazards & conditions
Flow 3: En Route & Obstacles
Step 1: Real-Time Monitor
• Detect community-reported obstacles
• Construction, broken sidewalks
• Blocked ramps
Step 2: Alert & Reroute
• "Obstacle ahead — rerouting"
• Auto-reroute around inaccessible segments
• No physical backtracking needed
Step 3: User Confirmation
• Accept reroute
• View reroute reason
Flow 4: Crossing Assistance
Step 1: Approaching
• Detect upcoming crossing
• Countdown timer
• "Safe to Cross" / "Please Wait"
Step 2: Multimodal Guidance
• Visual: large countdown
• Audio: verbal cues
• Haptic: vibration alerts
Step 3: Completion
• "Crossing completed safely"
• Reduce stress at high-risk moments
Flow 5: Arrival & Summary
Step 1: Arrival
• Reach destination
• Confirm arrival
Step 2: Journey Summary
• Distance, time
• Safety score
• Incline & crossing difficulty
Step 3: Reflection
• "How was your journey?"
• Simple feedback: 👍 😐 👎
Flow 6: Hazard Reporting
Step 1: Report Trigger
• Tap "Report Hazard"
• Report: stairs, blocked sidewalk, broken curb, unsafe crossing
Step 2: Location & Detail
• Auto-detect location
• Select hazard type
• Optional photo upload
Step 3: Submission
• Status: "Pending"
• Verified after multiple reports
• Maintain trust & accuracy
Flow 7: Community Map
Step 1: Open Map
• View nearby reports
• Filter by: hazard type, time, accessibility relevance
Step 2: Impact Feedback
• See contribution impact
• "Your report helped 12 people avoid this route"
Flow 8: Journey History
Step 1: View History
• View past trips
• Safety score, difficulty, notes
Step 2: Personalization
• Update mobility needs
• System adapts future routing
End State
User feels: Safer • More Confident • Independent

Uncertainty → Predictability → Independence
